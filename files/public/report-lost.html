Reporting Tips
  • Include brand name, model number, and serial number if known.
  • Note any unique identifying marks (stickers, scratches, engravings).
  • Upload a photo if you have one — it increases chances of recovery.
  • Be specific about the location and time.
  • Check with nearby offices and security desks first.
Important
  • For lost IDs or documents, notify the admin office immediately.
  • If you lost something valuable, also file a security report.
  • Your contact info is kept private and shared only with admins.
