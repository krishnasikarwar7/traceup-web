Submission Tips
  • Take a clear photo of the item before submitting.
  • Hand over IDs and documents to the security office directly.
  • Note the exact location where you found it — even nearby landmarks.
  • Don't share item details publicly — let the owner verify through the portal.
  • Check if there's already a matching lost report on the dashboard.
